Privacy policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

This website belongs to The Assist, the personal styling service run by Marcus Ervin. This page explains what happens to the details you share here. In short: the only personal information the site collects is what you type into the consultation form, and it is used only to reply to you.

What we collect

When you send the consultation form, we receive your first and last name, your email address, and anything else you choose to add: a phone number, the package you're interested in, and your message.

The site has no accounts, no cookies, and no analytics or advertising trackers. Its fonts are served from this site rather than from Google.

How we use it

Marcus uses your details to reply to your request, arrange a consultation, and provide the services you ask for. Your details are never sold, and they are not used for marketing unless you ask for that.

Who handles it

  • Web3Forms receives the form and forwards it to Marcus's email inbox. See the Web3Forms privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any web host, it may record technical details such as your IP address in its server logs. See the GitHub privacy statement.

How long we keep it

Marcus keeps your messages only as long as he needs them to respond to you and to provide any service you book.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the details you've sent at any time. Send the request through the consultation form or message Marcus on Instagram or LinkedIn.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
